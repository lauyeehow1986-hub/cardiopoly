CLINICAL PROTOCOL & BILLING GUIDE

CARDIOPOLY

Where evidence-based medicine meets bureaucratic reality.

1. The Two Game Modes

Mode A: Clinical Mastery (Strategic Gamer Mode):
Compete to complete your Faction Victory Objective (GDMT adherence, Procedural RVUs, Department Monopolies, or Audit Clawbacks). Vital signs are a survival hazard: crossing SBP ≥ 200 mmHg or BMI ≥ 70 kg/m² causes a Code Blue Hospitalization (M&M Peer Review admission, vitals reset, and 1,500 WP resuscitation fine). If global waste reaches the Ministry Audit Freeze cap (10k/30k WP), the match ends and the highest Clinical Prestige score wins!

Mode B: Bureaucratic Satire (Classic Parody Mode):
The classic inverted race to medical absurdity where the first player to cross SBP ≥ 200 mmHg, WP ≥ 10,000, or BMI ≥ 70 kg/m² wins via glorious medical excess.

2. Interactive Clinical Case Vignettes

Landing on any clinical suite triggers a real patient case with cross-disciplinary complexities (Cardiorenal eGFR contrast checks, Endocrinology SGLT2i/GLP-1 guidelines, Heart Team CABG vs. PCI). Choose between:

  • Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT): Evidence-based care, low complications, awards Academic Clinician points.
  • High-Margin Procedural Intervention: Maximizes Interventionalist RVUs & hospital facility fees, but risks contrast nephropathy and audit scrutiny.
  • Cost-Containment / Defensive Observation: Optimizes 23-hour observation bed turnover for the Hospital CEO.

3. Department Monopolies & Upgrades

Own all suites in a color group to collect double rent! Once a monopoly is secured, you can spend WP to upgrade suites across 3 tiers: Tier 1: Clinical Fellow (1.5x), Tier 2: Subspecialty Attending (2.2x), and Tier 3: Flagship Heart Institute (3.5x + mandatory clinical challenge for rivals).

4. International Healthcare Systems

Play under 9 authentic national models: Singapore 3M (IP 5% co-pay riders, MOH fee benchmarks, Healthier SG), US Commercial FFS, UK NHS NICE, Sweden SWEDEHEART, Japan Chuikyo, South Korea HIRA, Taiwan Global Budget, Malaysia, and Indonesia BPJS.
