CLINICAL PROTOCOL & BILLING GUIDE

CARDIOPOLY

Where lifestyle neglect meets high-margin metal.

1. The Clinical Mission

Welcome to modern hospital cardiology! You compete against 3 bot opponents — Fat Slob, Lazy Larry, and Sloppy Susan — around a 40-tile hospital circuit. Nobody plays to "win" — you are here to witness the glorious escalation of medical bureaucracy, defensive diagnostics, and lifestyle neglect.

2. Vital Signs & Waste Points (No Money!)

Instead of cash, every decision escalates three vital metrics:

  • SBP (Systolic Blood Pressure): Rises with hospital stress, sodium-laden cafeteria cookies, and surprise billing audits.
  • BMI (Body Mass Index): Fluctuates wildly between miracle GLP-1 shortages, bariatric interventions, and trans-fat lobbying.
  • WP (Waste Points): Acquiring hospital departments, ordering defensive tests, or visiting rival units (+100 WP) adds directly to your Waste Points score.

3. Acquiring Suites, Group Monopolies & Runaway Auctions

Land on an unowned Cardiology property (e.g., Oculostenotic space or Troponin cubicle) and you may claim it for its base WP price.

Full Department Monopoly (Double Rent): Landing on a property owned by another player increases your Waste score by 100 WP (along with raising your SBP and BMI). When a player owns all properties in a color group, the rent doubles (200 WP)!

If a player declines to buy: An instant 10-second group auction begins! The price counter ticks rapidly upward from the base price to 1,000 WP. Anyone can step in and hit "Buy" as the waste escalates — but if the counter reaches 1,000 WP, bidding stops and the player who originally landed on the property is saddled with it at the maximum 1,000 WP!

4. Backroom Department Trades & Dice

Swap hospital turf one-for-one with bots in the Trades panel below, or respond when bots propose a trade. Tapping the Dice Box at the top rolls the dice and steers tokens into maximum satirical drama.

5. Clinical Milestones & The Finish Line

As medical waste and physical decline mount, clinical milestones will trigger — impacting players individually as they reach clinical milestones.

The game reaches its climax and ends when any player crosses one of the three terminal milestones:

  • Systolic Blood Pressure (SBP) ≥ 200 mmHg: Hypertensive transcendence!
  • Waste Points (WP) ≥ 10,000 WP: Bureaucratic mastery!
  • Body Mass Index (BMI) ≥ 70 kg/m²: Morbid glory!
